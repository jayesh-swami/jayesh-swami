This is a continuation of the series of courses I am doing to revisit and solidify my mathematical foundation for machine learning.

I audited this course and hence did not have access to the graded assignments and labs. To cover it up, I generated quizzes for each lesson of the course using Claude. These quizzes were quite good and covered the topics in greater depth, which I think was lacking in the course.

I will share the links to the generated quizzes here, as I feel my true learning happened through them, and the course served as a milestone check -

Overall, I liked the course structure but would have appreciated more mathematical depth.
