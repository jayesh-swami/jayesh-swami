I took this course to begin my journey into machine learning. I wanted to revisit the fundamentals I learned in my bachelor's and learn a bit more about linear algebra along the way.

This course was amazing! I am glad I chose it, as Mr. Strang teaches linear algebra like a poet. He made the course easy to follow, and the exercises were really good as well. 

Since this was my first academic course after working as a professional for 4 years, I am glad I picked the right course to get back into learning!
